Field notes / short reads

Three checks before you commit.

Practical articles for SuperBuy clothing finds, sneaker research and QC photo review.

01 / SuperBuy sneaker spreadsheet

Sneaker sizing: start with the foot, then the label.

Measure both feet while standing and use the longer measurement as a starting point. Compare it with the exact listing’s size chart.

Check whether a measurement describes foot length, insole length or outsole length; these are different measurements. A familiar EU or US label alone cannot establish fit.

  • Keep measurements in the same units.
  • Ask about width when the chart only gives length.
  • Confirm the selected model and size together.

02 / SuperBuy clothing finds

Clothing research: compare a garment you already wear.

Lay a well-fitting garment flat and record its chest width, shoulder width, sleeve length and total length. Compare these with the seller’s chart using the same measurement method.

Check fabric composition, lining and intended cut. For sets, compare the top and bottom separately.

  • Distinguish flat width from body circumference.
  • Check hoodie room for layering.
  • Confirm waist and rise for pants or shorts.

03 / SuperBuy QC photo checklist

QC photographs: useful evidence with clear limits.

Request clear views of the front, back, labels, seams, soles and hardware as relevant. Look for differences between the ordered option and the photographed item.

Photographs can reveal visible details but cannot establish authenticity, long-term durability or fit. Ask for clarification before accepting an unresolved discrepancy.

  • Review both sides and close-up details.
  • Separate lighting differences from visible defects.
  • Keep a record of questions and responses.

Straight answers

Before you browse.

Know what this guide provides, where its links lead and which details need independent confirmation.

What is a SuperBuy spreadsheet?

A SuperBuy spreadsheet is an organized collection of product references used to research items for a shopping-agent workflow. Entries may include source links, categories, seller prices, measurements and photographs. Treat each entry as a research lead and verify its current details.

Is this an official SuperBuy website?

No. This is an independent editorial guide. It is not operated by or affiliated with SuperBuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, Hermès or Dior. General browsing links lead to Cafans, while category links open the specified Cafans category pages.

Can I download a spreadsheet from this page?

No downloadable spreadsheet is hosted here. This page provides category navigation and practical research guidance. Browse on Cafans to review the information available there.

Do product photographs prove authenticity?

No. A product photograph or QC image does not establish authenticity. Check the seller’s provenance, documentation and return terms, and use a qualified authentication service when appropriate.

Does a listed item price include delivery?

Do not assume it does. Check the item price, domestic delivery, applicable service and payment fees, international freight and any destination charges separately. This page does not quote current prices or shipping rates.

How should I compare shoe and clothing sizes?

Compare measurements with the exact seller’s chart. For footwear, distinguish foot length from insole and outsole length. For clothing, compare a well-fitting garment measured using the same method. Size labels alone are insufficient.
